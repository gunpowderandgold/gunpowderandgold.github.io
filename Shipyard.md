
## Ship Archetypes
#### Yawl 

Small rowboats, sometimes equipped with rudimentary sails. Used to transport goods and people between ships and shore. 

Cannot carry weapons. Disabled on any successful hit. 

**Masts** -; **Speed** 1; **Gun Decks** -; **Cargo** 5; **Crew** -

---
#### Cutter 

Small, fast vessels popular with smugglers and fishermen. Sometimes mounted with swivel cannons.

Can sail against the wind.

**Masts** 1; **Speed** 3;  **Gun Decks** -; **Cargo** 5; **Crew** Tiny

---
#### Schooner 

Small, fast merchant vessels. Prioritises cargo space over firepower. 

Carries only swivels and deck guns, which hit with -2 lethality. 

**Masts** 1; **Speed** 3; **Gun Decks** -; **Cargo** 20; **Crew** Small

---
#### Sloop

Small, fast vessels popular with pirates and navies. 

**Masts** 1; **Speed** 3; **Gun Decks** 1; **Cargo** 10; **Crew** Small

---
#### Brigantine 

Fast, medium-sized vessels combining speed and firepower. Popular with merchants, pirates and navies. 

**Masts** 2; **Speed** 3; **Gun Decks** 2; **Cargo** 20; **Crew** Medium 

---
#### Fluyt

Slow, spacious merchant vessels, with limited firepower and manoeuvrability. 

**Masts** 2; **Speed** 1; **Gun Decks** 1; **Cargo** 45; **Crew** Small 

---
#### Hoy 

Medium-sized barges used as transport ships and gunboats. 

Carries a Mortar if used as a gunboat, otherwise carries only deck guns (-2 lethality).

**Masts** 2; **Speed** 2; **Gun Decks** -; **Cargo** 15; **Crew** Small 

---
#### Indiaman 

Massive merchantmen, lightly armed and slow. 

**Masts** 3; **Speed** 1; **Gun Decks** 1; **Cargo** 70; **Crew** Large 

---
#### Frigate 

Large military vessels combining speed and firepower. One of the most common navy ships in the Caribbean. 

**Masts** 3; **Speed** 3; **Gun Decks** 3; **Cargo** 30; **Crew** Large 

----
#### Galleon 

Archaic sailing vessels, with thick reinforced sides and heavy firepower. 

Modifies Damage Checks by -2 (hull armour)

**Masts** 3; **Speed** 1; **Gun Decks** 3; **Cargo** 60; **Crew** Large 

---
#### Man-o-War

Mobile artillery batteries, serving as capitol ships for the great powers. 

**Masts** 3; **Speed** 1; **Gun Decks** 4; **Cargo** 40; **Crew** Massive 

---

## Upgrades 

Ships may equip as many upgrades as they have Masts. 

- **Carronade** +4 lethality when fired at **Close Action**
- **Mortar** Fireable at Long Shot range
- **Chase Cannons** Fireable when pursuing 
- **Stern Cannons** Fireable when fleeing
- **Swivel Cannons** Small deck-cannons, +4 lethality against individuals 
- **Crew Quarters** Exchange 10 cargo spaces to increase crew size to next tier 
- **Overgunned** Exchanges 5 cargo spaces to increase gun decks by 1. 
- **Copper Bottom** +1 speed 
- **Reinforced Hull** -2 to Damage Checks 
- **Armour Plated** -4 to Damage Checks, -1 to Speed
