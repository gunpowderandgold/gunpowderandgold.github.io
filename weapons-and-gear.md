
### Inventory 

Characters have a total of 10 inventory slots. Characters carrying more are *disadvantaged* in combat and roll d10 for initiative. 

***Bulky*** items take up two slots and are typically two-handed or awkward to carry. 

### Armour 

Wearing armour provides a modifier to your *Injury Check*, but it is **Bulky** and obstructs movement. 

**Leather Jerkin** -2 to Injury Checks. Bulky. 
**Steel Curaiss** -4 to Injury Checks. Bulky. 

### Melee Weapons 

Weapons with a higher **reach** score have an advantage to combat in the open, but a disadvantage in cramped conditions. 

**Lethality** is applied to *Injury Checks* caused by the weapon. 

| Weapon                | Reach | Lethality |
| --------------------- | ----- | --------- |
| Fist                  | 0     | -4        |
| Stone                 | 0     | -2        |
| Cudgel                | 1     | 0         |
| Dagger                | 0     | 1         |
| Stiletto              | 0     | 2         |
| Hatchet               | 1     | 2         |
| Cutlass               | 1     | 3         |
| Rapier                | 2     | 3         |
| Boarding Axe (Bulky)  | 2     | 4         |
| Boarding Pike (Bulky) | 4     | 3         |

### Firearms

Firearms can be fired once during a violent altercation, requiring a moment of calm to reload. Those with training can spend a turn to reload. 

Firearms have a disadvantage when targeted beyond their range. 

| Weapon           | Range        | Lethality |
| ---------------- | ------------ | --------- |
| Flintlock Pistol | Short        | 2         |
| Musket           | Long         | 4         |
| Blunderbuss      | Short/Medium | 5/2       |
