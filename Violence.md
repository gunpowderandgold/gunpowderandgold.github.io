
### Initiative 

Roll a d20 for each individual or group involved in the combat. Write these down and resolve, highest first. Repeat each round. 

### Dis/Advantage

When making a roll, note down all factors which afford a Disadvantage or Advantage. These should be easy to spot in natural language, but might also be called out in the text. 

Advantages and Disadvantages cancel each other out. Each remaining net advantage or disadvantage represent an additional d6 added to the dice pool. 

For d20 rolls (shooting, skill checks), roll a d20 normally. Then roll all d6s, and add the highest result to the d20 result for an advantage, or subtract for a disadvantage. 

For 2d6 rolls (melee) roll all d6s together, taking the two highest if you are advantaged or the two lowest if you are disadvantaged. 

### Experience

Combat experience is compared against your opponent. The difference between the two is added to the more skilled combatant's melee roll. 

- Untrained (0
- Drilled / Tough Guy (1)
- Trained / Blooded (2)
- Veteran (3)
- Elite / Hardened (4)
- Trained with Masters / Natural Born Killer (5)

### Shooting 

To shoot someone, roll 16+ on a d20. If hit, make an *Injury Check*. A natural 1 means you have run out of powder and shot. 

Roll with **Advantage** if the target is not in cover or moving. 

### Melee 

Both combatants roll 2d6. If multiple opponents engage a single person, all roll 2d6. The highest roll is taken for the team with the numerical advantage. 

A weapon's reach will give **Advantage** to the combatant with the higher score. 

Compare the combatant's rolls 

| Difference | **Consequences**                                      |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-1        | Both participants make an *Injury Check*              |
| 2-3        | The defeated makes an *Injury Check*                  |
| 4+         | The defeated is *Injured* and makes an *Injury Check* |

### Grappling 

Combatants may elect to grapple instead of causing an Injury Check. When grappling, role d6 and consult the table below. A combatant may do any of the actions listed at the value rolled or below (e.g rolling a 4 means actions 1 - 4 can be done). Skilled grapplers add +1. A character using a weapon larger than a dagger rolls 1d3. 

1. Shove or Go to Ground 
2. Slam 
3. Throw
4. Joint Lock 
5. Pin 
6. Choke 

**Shoving** propels the victim away. If they collide with an object, there is a 3 in 6 chance they fall, requiring an Injury Check. 

**Going to Ground** means both combatants hit the ground. The victor gets advantage on the next round of combat against their opponent. 

**Slams** strike the grappled person against an object. This triggers an Injury Check. A character with a weapon may instead make a pommel strike. The grappler also gets advantage on the next round of combat against the grappled opponent. 

**Throws** launch the grappled combatant. They have a 5 in 6 chance of falling, and anyone they hit has a 3 in 6 chance. Those falling take Injury Checks. 

**Joint Locks** disable a limb, usually one holding a weapon. A break can be forced on a 2 in 6, which is an automatic injury. 

**Pins** disable the victim totally, disarming them in the process. 

**Chokes** Down the grappled combatant, knocking them out for d6x10 minutes. 

### Injuries

To determine the severity of an injury, roll a d20. Each Injury already sustained adds +2. Armour modifies this roll. 

| d20  | Result                  |
| ---- | ----------------------- |
| 1-7  | Flesh wound. No effect. |
| 8-13 | Injured.                |
| 14+  | Down.                   |
### Down

After the violence is over, roll d20 for each person Down. Add +2 for each Injury. On a 16+, they are dead. On 11-15 they are seriously injured, requiring d6 weeks to recover. Otherwise, they are unconscious for 1d6x10 minutes. 

### Luck 

Players have a maximum of three Luck dice. Use these dice to add an Advantage. 

Alternatively, they can be rolled to improve or diminish a consequences. A roll of 1 or 2 means there is no effect. On a 3 or 4 the consequence is moved one step (e.g Down to Injured). On a 5 or 6 the consequence is moved two steps (e.g Down to Flesh Wound). Luck is always spent after rolling. 

### Explosives 

Resolve as Shooting against all in the blast radius. 
- Those in the **kill zone** automatically receive d6 injuries. They add +8 to be Shot and +6 to the Injury Check. 
- Those in the **shrapnel zone** automatically receive 1d4 injuries. They add +6 to be Shot and +4 to the Injury Check. 
- Those in the **tertiary zone** add +4 to be Shot and +2 to the Injury Check. 

### Shooting a charging person

Those with a drawn firearm can try to shoot an individual charging at them out of initiative order. Attempting this means they forgo their turn. 
- If they are already pointing the weapon, they have a 4 in 6 chance of getting a shot off, resolved normally 
- If attempting to draw and shoot a pistol, they have a 2 in 6 chance getting a shot off, resolved with disadvantage 
- If attempting to draw and shoot a musket, they have a 1 in 6 chance of getting a shot off, resolved with disadvantage 

### Non-Violence

When common sense is insufficient to resolve an issue, roll d20 over a difficulty number. This roll is subject to Dis/Advantages.
