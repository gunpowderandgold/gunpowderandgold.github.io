
Travelling between two points on a map is known as a passage. Passages are measured in days, and have different qualities depending on their location and usage: 

**Coastal Waters** link islands together in the same chain, and tend to be used by fishermen, local traders and the occasional patrol. 

**Trade Routes** link major ports together, and are frequented by larger merchantmen - and the pirates who prey on them. 

**Deep Waters** are navigated by only the bravest or most foolhardy of captains. Who knows what lurks in the depths? 
### Supplies

A ship can carry three days' supplies for the entire crew per cargo slot. Supplies are consumed automatically. A captain can order his crew onto *half rations*, consuming supplies at half the normal rate. 

Crews (and characters) who go a day without eating are *famished*, which carries the same penalties as an injury. An injury and morale check must be made on every third day without food. 

### Travel Events  

Once per day, roll on the following table. 

| d6  | Travel Event      |
| --- | ----------------- |
| 1   | Active Encounter  |
| 2   | Passing Encounter |
| 3   | Hazard            |
| 4   | Ship Problem      |
| 5-6 | No event          |
