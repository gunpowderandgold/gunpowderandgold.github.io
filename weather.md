Weather in the Caribbean may be simulated using the following method: first, place a marker in the centre of the hex-flower below. Then roll a d6 once at the start of each day of travel moving the marker across the hexes in the direction shown and recording the weather indicated by each. If the marker moves off the edge of the chart, it should wrap around onto the opposite side. The exception to this rule is the edges marked with an X; the marker cannot cross these edges and instead it should stay put for that day.
