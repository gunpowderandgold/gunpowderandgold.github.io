
### Weather Gauge 

If the Weather Gauge is uncertain, both ships roll d20 and add their speed. Highest roll wins. Ships with the Weather Gauge go first in shooting rounds. Do this each round. 

### Range 

- **Horizon** Out of range for all weapons 
- **Long Shot** Long Guns fire with a Disadvantage 
- **Close Action** Standard Long Gun range
- **Boarding** Long Guns hit with Advantage. 

### Manoeuvring

Both ships roll 2d6, subject to Dis/Advantage, and compares results on the table below. The faster ship automatically gains an advantage, as does the captain with the higher skill. 

| Difference | Consequence                                              |
| ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-1        | Neither ship outmanoeuvres the other                     |
| 2-3        | The victor may move a Range Band                         |
| 4+         | The victor may move a Range Band or perform a **Gambit** |
### Gambits 

Captains may attempt a Gambit instead of moving a range band. 

**Broadside** lines the ship's cannons up to take full advantage of a gun deck. Successful hits deal +4 to Damage Checks. 

**Raking** positions a ship to the stern or bow of its opponent. It cannot be hit by its enemy's guns, unless they have stern or bow chasers. 

**Grappling** means both ships are entangled together. Can only be attempted at Boarding range. Requires a Gambit to break free. 

### Shooting 

To shoot a ship, roll 16+ on a d20 per gun deck. If hit, make a Damage Check. A natural 1 means the ship has run out of shot and powder. 

### Damage Checks 

To determine the severity of the damage, roll d20 and consult the table below. Each Direct Hit the ship already has adds +2 to the roll. Broadsides add +4 to the roll. 

| Consequence               | One Mast | Two Masts | Three Masts |
| ------------------------- | -------- | --------- | ----------- |
| Glancing shot. No effect. | 1-7      | 1-10      | 1-15        |
| Direct Hit.               | 7-12     | 11-15     | 16-20       |
| Disabled.                 | 13+      | 16+       | 21+         |
A direct hit is not just an abstract measurement of damage. The referee should endeavour to make them real: shattered masts, blasted rudders, raked gun decks. These may be 'called' ahead of a Shooting roll. 

### Disabled 

Each round, roll d20 for each disabled ship. Add +2 per Direct Hit. 

On a 16+, it is destroyed, rapidly sinking. On a natural 20, the magazine explodes. 

On an 11-15, it will sink in d6 rounds. Otherwise it will sink in d6 hours without repairs. 

### Crew Stations

All ships require a **helmsman** to manoeuvre and a **gunner** to fire Long Guns. Additional characters may assist: 

- **Rigging** Gives advantage on manoeuvre rolls (max 2)
- **Gundecks** Gives advantage on shooting rolls (max 2)
- **Additional Guns** Fire any swivel cannons, mortars or chase cannons the ship has
- **Sharpshooting** Fire personal weapons at individuals 
- **Other** Motivate crew, wield occult powers, cower in the hold

Crew Stations must be assigned ahead of each manoeuvre phase, and can reset each Round. 

If a ship takes a Direct Hit, any crew in that area must make an Injury Check. 

### Boarding 

Both crews roll 2d6 and add Dis/Advantage. The larger crew gains an automatic Advantage, as does the more skilled crew. 

Individual characters may contribute, which may be resolved as melee combat before the Boarding roll. 

| Difference | Consequence                                                |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0-1        | Both crews make a Morale Check                             |
| 2-3        | The defeated makes a Morale Check                          |
| 4+         | The defeated takes **casualties** and makes a Morale Check |
### Morale 

Roll a d20. If the crew has already sustained Casualties, it adds +2 for each. If testing outside of combat, crews may be Shaken instead.

| d20  | Result                |
| ---- | --------------------- |
| 1-6  | Hold Fast. No effect. |
| 7-12 | Casualties or Shaken  |
| 13+  | Routed or Mutiny      |

Casualties or Shaken give a crew Disadvantage to ship actions. These statuses can be removed by Shore Leave.
